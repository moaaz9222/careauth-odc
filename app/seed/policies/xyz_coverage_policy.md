# XYZ Health Coverage Policy
*Sample policy — illustrative only.*

## §1.1 General Coverage
XYZ Health covers a broad range of inpatient and outpatient services. Our plans prioritize preventative care and evidence-based medicine. Network participation is required to maximize benefit coverage.

## §1.2 Standard Limitations
Services provided outside the approved network without an authorized exception will be subject to higher out-of-pocket costs or may be completely denied. 

## §1.3 Emergency Services
Emergency services are covered worldwide. Prior authorization is not required for emergency care, but the plan must be notified within 48 hours of admission.
