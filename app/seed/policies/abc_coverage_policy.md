# ABC Insurance Coverage Policy
*Sample policy — illustrative only.*

## §1.1 General Provisions
ABC Insurance provides coverage for medically necessary services that are generally accepted in the medical community. All covered services must be performed by in-network providers unless an emergency exception applies or out-of-network benefits are specifically selected in the plan. 

## §1.2 Exclusions
Services considered experimental, investigational, or unproven are not covered. Routine physicals, cosmetic surgery, and alternative medicine such as acupuncture are excluded under standard plans unless a special rider is attached.

## §1.3 Definitions
- **Medically Necessary:** Healthcare services or supplies needed to diagnose or treat an illness, injury, condition, disease, or its symptoms and that meet accepted standards of medicine.
- **Prior Authorization:** A decision by the health insurer or plan that a healthcare service, treatment plan, prescription drug or durable medical equipment is medically necessary.

## §1.4 Claim Submission
All claims must be submitted within 180 days of the date of service. Claims missing appropriate diagnostic codes or clinical justification may be delayed or denied pending review.
