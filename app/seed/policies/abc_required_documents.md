# ABC Insurance Required Documents Matrix
*Sample policy — illustrative only.*

## §6.1 Required Documentation
All prior authorization requests must be accompanied by sufficient clinical documentation to support medical necessity. Different services require different baseline documents.

For Advanced Imaging (including MRI Brain under ABC Gold PPO):
1. **insurance_card**: A valid, readable copy of the member's insurance card.
2. **physician_order**: A signed order from the referring or treating physician.
3. **clinical_notes**: Detailed physician progress notes demonstrating the medical necessity of the procedure, including symptom duration and prior treatments tried.
4. **prior_imaging_report**: Previous imaging report (e.g., prior CT or MRI) to establish that the study is not duplicative or to provide baseline comparison.

## §6.2 Additional Information
ABC Insurance reserves the right to request additional documentation (such as lab results or specialized specialist referral letters) if the primary documentation is insufficient to make a coverage determination.
