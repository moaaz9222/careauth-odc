# XYZ Health Authorization Requirements
*Sample policy — illustrative only.*

## §2.1 Authorization Guidelines
XYZ Health requires prior authorization for elective surgeries, advanced imaging, and specialized outpatient therapies. 

## §2.2 Imaging Authorization
For CT and MRI studies, the referring provider must submit clinical notes and a valid physician order. Unlike other plans, XYZ Health generally requires documented conservative therapy for at least 6 weeks before approving advanced joint or spine imaging.

## §2.3 Submission Process
Requests must be submitted via the designated provider portal or an approved API integration. Incomplete requests will be returned to the provider as "Needs More Information".
