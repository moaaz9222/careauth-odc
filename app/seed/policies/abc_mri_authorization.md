# ABC Insurance MRI Authorization Policy
*Sample policy — illustrative only.*

## §4.1 Introduction
This section details the requirements and conditions under which ABC Insurance covers Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) studies.

## §4.2 MRI Authorization Criteria
Advanced imaging services, including magnetic resonance imaging of the brain, require prior authorization when performed in an outpatient setting. 

For ABC Gold PPO members requesting an MRI of the Brain (e.g., CPT 70551, 70552, 70553), the following criteria must be met:
1. Documented failure of conservative management for at least 4 weeks.
2. Referral from a treating physician detailing the symptoms (e.g., persistent headaches).

## §4.3 Medical Necessity
Imaging is not deemed medically necessary if symptoms are transient, or if a recent prior imaging study is already available and the clinical picture has not significantly changed. All requests must be supported by appropriate clinical context explaining why the scan is needed now.
